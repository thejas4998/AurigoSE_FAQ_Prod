Toyota Camry 2025 User Manual

Dashboard Controls
------------------

1. Start/Stop Button:
   Located on the right side of the steering column. Press once to enter accessory mode, twice for ignition ON, and hold with brake pedal to start engine.

2. Instrument Cluster:
   Displays speedometer, fuel level, odometer, and hybrid indicators.

3. Infotainment Touchscreen:
   - 9" Display with Navigation
   - Supports Bluetooth, USB, and voice commands
   - Customize settings through the “Settings” menu

4. Climate Control:
   - Dual-zone automatic temperature control
   - Front seat heaters (XLE & above)
   - Rear air vents

5. Steering Wheel Controls:
   - Left side: Audio & phone controls
   - Right side: Cruise control, lane-keeping assist, and MID navigation
