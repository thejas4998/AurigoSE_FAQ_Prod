Toyota Camry 2025 User Manual

Maintenance Schedule
---------------------

To maintain optimal performance, follow the maintenance schedule below.

Every 5,000 km / 6 months:
- Engine oil & filter change
- Tire rotation
- Brake inspection
- Fluid level check

Every 15,000 km / 12 months:
- Cabin air filter replacement
- Inspect cooling system
- Check battery condition

Every 30,000 km / 24 months:
- Engine air filter replacement
- Inspect suspension & steering
- Brake fluid replacement

Every 60,000 km / 48 months:
- Spark plug replacement
- Transmission fluid inspection
- Fuel system check

Note: Refer to the Toyota Service Booklet for full details on service intervals.
